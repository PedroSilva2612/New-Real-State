## Scope

One-page landing site for New Real State (src/pages/index.astro). Mode: Persuade. Audience: people buying, renting or selling a home, comparing agencies, often on a phone. Main action: Agenda una visita (the contact form, Netlify Forms).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site walks the visitor through a house, and each room is one step of buying a home. It refuses the category default of a search bar hero over a skyline with a grid of listing cards.

OWN-WORLD: Stone ground #dfdedb, graphite ink #26251e, one cobalt accent #0069ff taken from the unchanged logo. Red Hat Display at weight 400 with tight tracking for headings, Red Hat Text for body. Soft radius (8/12/20). Flat grounds, one dark band (Descuento) and a dark footer. Real photographs of houses and rooms in every section. A small blueprint floor plan in the tour panel marks the room you are in and the route from the front door.

STORY: The visitor understands what the agency does (buy, rent, sell, neighbourhoods), sees the five steps of a purchase as rooms of a house, believes it through the method (one adviser, homes visited first, fees in writing), sees the 50% sales-fee offer, and books a first visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Split hero. h1 "Inmobiliaria para comprar, vender o alquilar" on columns 1-5 at 72px over three lines, a one-sentence lead and the primary "Agenda una visita" plus "Ver el recorrido". On the right, columns 7-12, the owner's photograph of a curved glass house by the sea, full height, with one opaque float card holding the live offer (50% less in fees until 31 Dec). The fixed nav carries the same primary on the right line.

FORM: Signature interaction: photo-chapters pinned tour (#recorrido), five rooms uncovered one after another from the foot with a slight settle, each panel holding its step and the floor plan wayfinder. Second moment: word-highlight on the Sobre nosotros statement. Motion grammar: ease-out settles, a press on every button, no section entrance fades. Position on list: the brief's own picture (house tour), translated through design-taste-frontend's dealt photo-chapters moment. seed: none (pinned) "Quiero que sea una página bastante original, si pudieras crear una animación tipo recorriendo una casa"; visual direction delegated by the owner to design-taste-frontend ("design-taste-frontend para la dirección visual").

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
